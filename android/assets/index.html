Expected value assumption
Formulas and assumptions

For decimal odds d, implied q = 1/d. Let B be the sum of q. Normalized probability is q/B, fair decimal odds are B/q, overround is B−1 and theoretical margin is 1−1/B. With an explicit assumed probability p, net EV per unit is p × d − 1. Outcomes must be exhaustive and mutually exclusive. Fees, pushes and taxes are excluded.
